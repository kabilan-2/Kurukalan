Information

How the estimate is calculated

We assume an average yield of 6,000 kg of paddy per hectare (about 2,428 kg per acre). Your land is converted to hectares, multiplied by the yield, and divided by the container capacity of 2,500 kg. The result is rounded up to whole containers.

This is only an estimate. Actual yield depends on the variety, season and farming practice.

Tips for storing paddy

  • Dry the paddy well before storing it.
  • Clean the container and remove old grain and husk.
  • Keep containers off wet ground and away from standing water.
  • Check the grain regularly for insects, smell and moisture.

About franchise centers

Centers are local agricultural storage points run by franchise partners. The admin team reviews each center and gives franchise access before it is shown as active on the map.
